# Problem & Value Proposition

## Problem statement (from MP1 deck)

> **How might we shift high foot traffic into lasting environmental awareness, rather than just Instagram-able moments?**

Two linked problem areas:
1. **Apathy towards the environment** — visitors pass through nature spaces without developing deeper awareness; a visit becomes a photo op, not a learning experience
2. **Gap in entertainment technology** — interactive digital experiences are underused for making biodiversity education playful, persistent, memorable

| Current issue | Desired outcome |
|---|---|
| Short-lived social media engagement | Lasting environmental awareness |
| Instagram-able moments | Meaningful learning & interaction with nature |

## Value proposition

> **A gamified and sticky experience for tourists to learn about plants.**

- **Gamified** — game mechanics make plant discovery engaging
- **Sticky** — designed for repeated interaction and long-term retention
- **Educational** — species info attached to every avatar

## Target users

1. **Public visitors & tourists** at gardens, trails, attractions — accessible entry point via scan/collect/battle
2. **Existing Sprout mobile users** — web companion with synced collections and stats
3. **Students & younger audiences** — already fluent in collection/avatar/progression loops
4. **B2B stakeholders** — gardens, parks, schools, museums, sustainability programmes, tourism boards, event organisers (showcase + partnership platform)

## How Sprout differs

| Compared with… | Sprout is… |
|---|---|
| Static nature signboards | Interactive, personalised, persistent |
| Plant-ID apps | Doesn't stop at identification — turns the plant into a game avatar |
| Generic games | Grounded in real-world nature interaction |
| Pure marketing sites | Both showcase **and** working platform |
| Conventional edu tools | Collection + battle + identity → self-driven learning |
